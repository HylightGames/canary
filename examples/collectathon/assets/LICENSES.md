## Provenance

- `quad.glb`, `box.glb`, `rgba2x2.png`, `tone-mono-8k.wav`: copied
  byte-for-byte from `engine/canary-assets/tests/fixtures/`. Those fixtures
  were generated by hand with a dependency-free Python script (no exporter,
  no toolchain behavior to drift) as documented in that directory's
  `README.md`:
  - `quad.glb`: one quad mesh (positions, texcoords, indices; no normals).
  - `box.glb`: one cube mesh in two primitives (normals + texcoords on the
    second).
  - `rgba2x2.png`: 2x2 8-bit RGBA (red, green / blue, white).
  - `tone-mono-8k.wav`: mono 16-bit PCM at 8000 Hz, 8 hand-picked frames.
- `room.json`: original to this example — the seed room (player, three
  shards, goal) as an `AuthoredDocument` with `entity.<local>` sections.
- `guest.wat`: original to this example — the Tier A skeleton guest,
  shaped after the WAT patterns in
  `engine/canary-plugin-api/src/tier_a/tests.rs`.
